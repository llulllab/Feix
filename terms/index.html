Feix Terms of Service

Feix is operated by Firat Ilim, entrepreneur individuel, trading as Llull Lab / Studio Llull (SIREN 988 721 320, 61 rue de Lyon, 75012 Paris, France). Feix is a service for professional customers.

1. The service

Feix builds complete custom digital systems from your brief: interface, automation, database, hosting and delivery, as described at feix.app. Two delivery modes exist: Feix-managed (we host and operate your system) and self-hosted (we build and deploy on your infrastructure).

2. The pilot

We build first. You receive a working system and evaluate it in your own context for one week. If you adopt it, we agree a scope-based project price. If you do not adopt it, the system is removed, your project data is deleted (Privacy Policy, Section 7), and no invoice is issued.

3. Accounts and access

Access is invite-based. Keep your access token confidential; sessions may be revoked on abuse.

4. Acceptable use

No unlawful content or use, no systems whose primary purpose is deception, surveillance of individuals, or processing of special-category data without a prior written agreement.

5. Your content and intellectual property

Your brief and your system's data are yours. Upon payment for an adopted system, the delivered system (interface files and workflow definitions) is licensed to you for unrestricted business use, including export and self-hosting; there is no vendor lock-in. Generic building blocks, internal tooling and know-how remain ours. White-label terms are agreed per project.

6. Computational generation: no guarantee of fitness beyond evaluation

Systems are generated computationally and reviewed before delivery. You evaluate the system during the pilot; adoption confirms it meets your requirements as delivered. We do not guarantee outcomes beyond the delivered functionality.

7. Hosting and maintenance

Feix-managed systems include 90 days of hosting after adoption; afterwards a maintenance plan or migration to your infrastructure. Uptime and response targets are defined by the chosen maintenance plan.

8. Payment

Scope-based project price agreed after the pilot; maintenance plans billed monthly. Prices exclude VAT where applicable (franchise en base).

9. Privacy and data protection

The Privacy Policy at feix.app/privacy applies. Customers whose Feix-managed system processes personal data of their own users can request a DPA (GDPR Art. 28).

10. Availability

We aim for continuous availability but do not guarantee uninterrupted service outside agreed maintenance-plan targets.

11. Liability

Aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months preceding the event, except for liability that cannot be limited under French law.

12. Termination

Either party may end hosting/maintenance with 30 days notice. On termination, Section 7 of the Privacy Policy governs deletion and export.
