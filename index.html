Standard, or governed

Ordinary work runs simply. Sensitive work runs with a second set of checks.

Standard automation

Runs, and is recorded

The agent does the work and every step is logged, so you can always see what happened. This is the right level for most jobs.

Governed automation

Verified before it runs

For sensitive or regulated work, each action also passes an independent verification step and a recorded second opinion before it runs, with an audit trail you can export. Data stays in the European Union.

Governed automation is there for the work that carries real risk. You do not need it to start, and you can add it when the work calls for it.

Data and privacy

Built in the EU, and kept there.

For Feix-managed deployments, your brief and the generation process are processed within the European Union, and your system runs on EU-based infrastructure. Your content is not used to train models, and the underlying model vendor does not receive it.

EU processingGeneration and hosting run within the EU. Systems we build for managed hosting use EU model processing.
Yours to keepYour system and its data belong to you and are exportable at any time. No vendor lock-in.
Deleted if unusedIf the pilot ends without adoption, or on request, your data is removed from active systems immediately and expires from encrypted backups within two days.
Honest about the edgesTransactional email uses a non-EU provider under standard clauses, carrying your address and a request identifier, not your content.
